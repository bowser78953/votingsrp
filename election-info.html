Candidate Application

Applications are accepted only while candidate registration is open. Election Officials must review eligibility before a candidate is approved. Submitting an application does not make you a candidate.

Keep statements respectful, truthful, and about SRP. Real-world political content, personal attacks, fabricated claims, endorsements, promises of rewards, and private information are prohibited. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

Fair and Informed Participation

  • Make your own voting decision without pressure or intimidation.
  • Review candidates based on their qualifications, experience, conduct, and ability to serve.
  • Submit only the ballot you are authorized to cast. A submitted vote is final.
  • Real-world political parties, figures, endorsements, and propaganda are not part of SRP elections.
  • Your registration and eligibility must be confirmed through the official election process; this website cannot verify them.

Voting and Privacy Notice

This is a static website. It cannot securely verify voter eligibility, prevent duplicate ballots across devices, or maintain a shared confidential vote count. When voting is open, use only the official ballot link published by election administration. Do not treat this site's local preview, browser storage, or a submitted email draft as an official vote or result.

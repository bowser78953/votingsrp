Before you register

Your Google account ID and selected area are stored for this election round. The dashboard displays only a count; Firebase project administrators can access registration records and Google account information through Firebase. Your in-game and Discord usernames and notes stay in this browser. Registration is locked for the round and does not verify eligibility or prevent someone from using multiple Google accounts.

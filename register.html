Voter Registration

Enter your details, select your county and district, and sign in with Google to register.

Before you register

Google sign-in links your account ID and selected area to this election round and supports the registration counter and shared poll. Your account email and registration records can be viewed by Firebase project administrators; the site dashboard displays only the count. Your in-game and Discord usernames and notes stay in this browser. Your registration is locked for the round. Sign-in does not verify eligibility or prevent someone from using multiple accounts.
